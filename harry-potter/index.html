Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry

Hidden in the Scottish Highlands and invisible to Muggle eyes, Hogwarts has trained young witches and wizards for more than a thousand years. Moving staircases, talking portraits and an enchanted ceiling that mirrors the night sky hide secrets in every corridor.

First-years cross the Black Lake by lantern light, are sorted into one of four houses and spend seven years learning charms, potions, transfiguration and the art of flying a broomstick.

Gryffindor

“Where dwell the brave at heart”

Gryffindors are known for their courage, nerve and chivalry. They are the first to stand up for others, even when the odds are against them, and they rarely back down from a challenge. Their common room sits high in a tower, warmed by a crackling fire and guarded by the portrait of the Fat Lady.

“Cunning folk who use any means to achieve their ends”

“Wit beyond measure is man's greatest treasure”

Hufflepuff

“True and unafraid of toil”

Hufflepuffs are loyal, fair and hard-working. They treat everyone as equals, stand by their friends no matter what and are never afraid of honest work. Their cosy common room sits close to the kitchens, full of plants and round windows that let the sunlight in.
